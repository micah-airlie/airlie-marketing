About Airlie Marketing

Marketing for your business.

Who are we?

Airlie Marketing is a Charlottesville-based marketing company for local businesses: shops, restaurants, studios, service providers and local online stores. We work with owners who are good at what they do but don't have the time to market their business consistently.

We started Airlie with a simple belief: a good local business shouldn't lose customers to a competitor just because that competitor shows up first online. So we focus on what decides that: a complete Google profile, steady reviews, a website that turns visitors into customers, and consistent social media. That leaves you free to focus on running your business.

Meet the founder

Micah Scott

Founder, Airlie Marketing

Micah Scott is a Charlottesville native. He grew up just minutes from the University of Virginia campus and attended high school at The Covenant School. He graduated from Wofford College in 2022 with degrees in Business Economics and Government.

After graduation, he moved to Wilmington, North Carolina, where he worked at Live Oak Bank as a financial analyst for small owner-operator businesses, including veterinarians and dentists across the United States.

In 2025, Micah left Live Oak Bank to pursue a career in the Army, but was unable to serve his contract due to an injury sustained before shipping out. He returned to Charlottesville and founded Airlie Marketing to help small businesses, like the ones he worked with at Live Oak, reach more customers.
