About Airlie Marketing

Local marketing, built for businesses that do great work.

Who are we?

Airlie Marketing is a Charlottesville-based marketing company built for local service businesses. We collaborate with the owner/operators who are great at what they do but don't have time to consistently market in a way that increases visibility for new customers and retention for existing customers.

We started Airlie with a simple belief: a hardworking local service business shouldn't lose customers to a competitor just because that competitor showed up first online. So that's what we fix. We keep your Google listing active, build your credibility across AI and search engines, grow your reviews, and post for you consistently across social media — the things that actually make the phone ring — so you can stay focused on the work you do best.

What we value

Honesty.

No inflated promises, no confusing jargon, no long contracts. Just visible and consistent marketing.

Integrity.

We live and work here too. We treat your business like a neighbor, because it is.

Personalization.

We take the time to understand your business, so your marketing sounds like you, not a template.

Timeliness.

We handle the marketing so you can stay focused on your business. About 10–15 minutes a month from you is all it takes.

Meet the founder

Micah Scott

Founder, Airlie Marketing

Micah Scott is a Charlottesville native. He grew up just minutes from the University of Virginia campus and attended high school at The Covenant School. He graduated from Wofford College in 2022 with degrees in Business Economics and Government.

After graduation, he moved to Wilmington, North Carolina, where he worked at Live Oak Bank as a financial analyst for small owner-operator businesses, including veterinarians and dentists across the United States.

In 2025, Micah left Live Oak Bank to pursue a career in the Army, but was unable to serve his contract due to an injury sustained before shipping out. He returned to Charlottesville and founded Airlie Marketing, where he combines his passion and expertise for small businesses with the creativity and vision needed to build successful marketing campaigns.
