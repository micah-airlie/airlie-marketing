Frequently asked questions

Do I have to sign a long contract?
No. We ask for a 3-month start so the work has time to show results, then it's month-to-month. Cancel anytime.
How much of my time does this take?
About 10–15 minutes a month. Each month we send you the full content plan to approve in one sitting — then we handle the scheduling and day-to-day. Things like review replies follow simple guidelines we agree on up front, so you're not pinged over every little thing.
Isn't this just social media posts?
No — the core of what we do is keeping your Google Business Profile active and your reviews strong, because that's what actually drives local calls. Social posts keep you credible and top-of-mind on top of that. And on our Pro plan, we build your visibility across AI and search engines, so you become a recommended home-services pro — the name that comes up when a homeowner asks who to call.
What if I don't have photos?
No problem. We use professional graphics and tip-based posts, and we'll show you how to text us a couple of quick job photos when you have them.
Will the content actually sound like my business?
Yes. We start by learning your voice and services, and you approve the monthly plan. It reads like you, not generic.
What does it cost?
Plans start at $275/month. Most owners choose our $450 Grow plan. We'll help you pick on a free call.
