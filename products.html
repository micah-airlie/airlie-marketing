Google Profile Setup
$395
one time · 5 business days

Your Google Business Profile is the first thing most local customers see, and for plenty of them it's the only thing. This builds it out properly and points it at three places at once: Google search, the map pack, and the AI assistants people now ask for recommendations.

  • Claiming and verification handled, or the whole profile built from scratch if you don't have one
  • Categories chosen against what's actually ranking near you, not guessed

Local SEO Foundation
$550
one time · 10 business days

If your business name, address and phone number don't match everywhere they appear online, Google trusts all of them slightly less. This fixes that, then makes your website say plainly what you do and where you do it — to search engines, and to the AI assistants people increasingly ask instead.

  • An audit of your listings across the directories that actually feed Google, with mismatches corrected — however many listings that turns out to be
  • A keyword map — what people near you search for, matched to the pages that should be answering them
  • On-page work across your key pages: titles, headings, internal links, location language
  • LocalBusiness structured data, so search engines read your details directly instead of inferring them
  • Google Search Console set up, verified, sitemap submitted
  • A knowledge profile built for your business — what you do, where, for whom, and what makes you the answer to a specific question rather than a general one
  • An llms.txt file, so AI assistants have something accurate to quote about you
  • A baseline of what the main AI assistants say today when asked about your category in your area, and the same check run again after 30 days so you can see whether it moved
  • A written list of anything we turn up that's outside this scope

What we need: website admin access, your Google Business Profile, the areas you serve, and half an hour to talk about what you want to be known for. The on-page work covers up to six pages of your website — normally your home page, about, contact and your main services pages. If you've got more than that, we'll look first and quote. Everything else here — the listings audit, Search Console, the structured data, the AI work — isn't page-by-page and isn't capped. Work outside what's listed is $125 an hour, and we'd tell you before starting, not after.

One honest caveat on the AI part: nobody can promise an assistant recommends you. What this does is make sure that when one looks, it finds accurate and complete information instead of nothing.

Review Engine Setup
$500
one time · 5 business days

Most businesses without many reviews aren't bad at the work. They're bad at asking, because asking is awkward and easy to forget on the drive to the next job. This makes it part of the job instead.

  • A short review link and a printable QR code for invoices, trucks, counters and follow-up texts
  • Request templates for text and email, in your voice, timed for when customers are happiest
  • Reply templates for five-star, mixed and angry, so nobody's writing one of those cold
  • A one-page script your crew can actually say out loud without wincing
  • Review alerts routed to you, and a starting count so you can see the change

What we need: how you contact customers, who's doing the asking, and a feel for your tone.
